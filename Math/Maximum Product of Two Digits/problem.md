# LeetCode 3536 - Maximum Product of Two Digits

## Problem Statement

You are given a positive integer `n`.

Return the maximum product obtained by multiplying any two digits present in `n`.

## Example 1

**Input**

```text
n = 2736
```

**Output**

```text
42
```

**Explanation**

The digits of `2736` are `2`, `7`, `3`, and `6`. The maximum product is:

```text
7 × 6 = 42
```

## Example 2

**Input**

```text
n = 98
```

**Output**

```text
72
```

**Explanation**

The digits are `9` and `8`. Their product is:

```text
9 × 8 = 72
```

## Constraints

* `10 <= n <= 10^9`
